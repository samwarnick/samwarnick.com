The [first post](https://samwarnick.com/blog/hello-world/) on this blog was July 23, 2016. Ten years. I'm at the point in my life that ten years feels like a few months ago at the most. But it's actually a pretty long time it turns out.

When I bought and started samwarnick.com, I was still in college, living in Utah, and had just started an internship as a software developer. I didn't even have any kids[^1]. I can't quite remember why I started it when I did. Probably wanted a better home as I started looking for jobs[^2]. This blog was not my first. I had a handful of short-lived blogs and projects that I have no record of anymore. This is obviously the one that has stuck.

At the time, I was inspired by tutorial blogs. People like Wes Bos and Kent C. Dodds and others who were producing learning content on new technologies and stuff. I wanted to do that too. It didn't go very far. This blog is turned into the ramblings of an indecisive man who has no idea what he is doing. That's more fun and valuable right?

Perhaps fittingly, the first post was a Swift hello world. I was learning and teaching myself Swift and iOS development at the time, and one of my favorite features was that you could use emoji as variable names. I still use Swift today, and I still mostly like it. Still feels like a new language. I've released and sunset a few iOS apps. I'm [currently working on some now](https://samwarnick.com/blog/devlog-jun-29-jul-12-2026/). It's what I keep coming back to in my free time.

But I make my living mostly doing Angular and TypeScript. I've moved into some backend stuff too. Weird to think that I've been doing Angular for 10+ years. Over that time, it has gotten real close to being a framework I like. But I prefer Svelte and SvelteKit at the moment.

I have rebuilt this blog so many times. Jekyll on GitHub pages, Eleventy on Netlify, Blot.im, I had an Astro rewrite at some point, and now Eleventy on my own server. I'm probably missing some too. It's gone through about as many redesigns. I probably should have done a cool ten-year anniversary redesign[^3]. I do feel like another redesign is coming though. The whole beige look has been taken over by AI.

I'm not totally sure where I thought I would be in years when I started this, but I'm not sure I could ask for much more. I have an incredible wife, three incredibly clever kids, and a job that provides for us all and gives me pretty great work-life balance.

Here's to another ten years samwarnick.com. Who knows what it will be like in 2036, but I'm sure there will be plenty of ramblings on the way.

[^1]: But the first was on the way.
[^2]: Turned out I was lazy and did not actually look for jobs. Just went full-time at the company where I was an intern.
[^3]: Instead of a redesign, I will run an anniversary sale. Everything will still be free.
